A quick guide to the numbers +

Work W is the sum of task durations. With one processor, completion time equals W.

Span S is the longest dependency path. Even unlimited processors cannot finish faster than S.

Average parallelism W/S describes the computation’s available parallelism.

Speedup W/TP compares a schedule to its serial baseline.

Utilization W/(P × TP) is the fraction of processor time spent computing.

Lower bound max(W/P, S) is a limit on completion time. A schedule may not attain it.
